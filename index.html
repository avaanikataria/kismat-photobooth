Kismat se Diwali

Four photos. One keepsake. ✨

1. Upload your four photos

Choose four photos from your iPhone's photo library or Files. You can replace or reorder them before creating your card.

2. Your finished photo card

Your four photos are combined into one 1200 × 1800 PNG (4×6 inches at 300 pixels per inch).

On iPhone, tap Save / Share PNG → Print, select your printer, choose 4 × 6 inches and set 2 copies. This shares the PNG image—not the webpage.

Your photos are combined on this device. Nothing is uploaded to a server. Keep the final PNG saved until printing is complete.
